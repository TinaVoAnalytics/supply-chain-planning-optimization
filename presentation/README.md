# 📈 Business Analysis Presentation

**Project #4 — Supply Chain Planning & Operations Intelligence** 

This presentation communicates the key findings, operational risks, optimization trade-offs, and management recommendations from an end-to-end manufacturing S&OP planning analytics project.

## 🎯 Presentation Focus

- **Business Problem:** Demand, capacity constraints, and resource allocation challenges.
- **Capacity & Bottlenecks:** Aggregate headroom versus localized machine-period constraints.
- **Demand & Allocation:** Modeled fulfillment, workload concentration, and resource dependency.
- **Scenario Analysis:** Min Processing, Min Overtime, and Threshold Capacity trade-offs.
- **Business Risks & SWOT:** Potential planning risks, root-cause hypotheses, and diagnostic priorities.
- **Recommendations & Decision Support:** Corrective actions and scenario-based planning strategies.

## 💡 Key Management Message

**Aggregate capacity does not guarantee usable capacity at the right machine and planning period.**

The analysis demonstrates how different optimization objectives affect processing requirements, implied overtime, and machine-level workload distribution, supporting more informed planning decisions.
